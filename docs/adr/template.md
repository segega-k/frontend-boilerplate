## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, constraints, facts from research or incidents. Two to six sentences.

## Decision

What we do, stated as rules a reader can check. Keep it short; link to the rule file or doc that enforces it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we must now maintain, and how the decision is enforced (lint, check,
hook, or `manual`).

- Option: why it was not chosen.
